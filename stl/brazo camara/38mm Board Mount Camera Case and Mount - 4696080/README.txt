38mm Board Mount Camera Case and Mount by stevnkrn on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4696080

Summary:
This camera case was designed to house a 38mm square circuit board camera where the camera lens is located in the center of the circuit board.  I used the parts to mount two Spinel UC10MPC cameras used to capture golf swing videos in a golf simulator, although I believe the case and mount will work for many cameras of a similar size and shape for other applications.  The circuit board camera is secured in the case by simply by snapping on the rear cover.  No screws are needed.The tripod and mounting bracket threads come from the use of a 1/4-20 nut (in the housing) and 3/4" long bolt (for the mounting bracket knob).  The nut and bolt cavities are designed for press fitting, but whether or not that works for everyone will depend on print quality and variation in 3D print tolerances.For wall mounting I used Velcro on the back of the mount because I have carpeted padding on the wall adjacent to my hitting area. To mount on a firmer surface I would have used 3M Command Strips. Rev. 1  Added case with .65" diameter lens holeRev. 2 Added case with for 1.7mm fisheye lens
